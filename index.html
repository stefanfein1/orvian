About Us

Swiss planning policy directs growth towards land that is already developed. Much of the remaining potential lies in former industrial sites and in buildings that have outlived their original purpose.

Our work on these sites starts with the question of use: what a site or building can become, and who will live or work there. Design and planning follow once that is settled.

Where an existing structure can be kept, we examine conversion alongside new construction. Future operators and the municipality are involved early, so that the plans reflect their requirements from the outset.
